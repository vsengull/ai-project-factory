# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level
of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste,
color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and
healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respect differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Accept responsibility, apologize to those affected by mistakes, and learn from the experience.
- Focus on what is best for the community, not only individual preferences.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or attention, trolling, insulting or derogatory
comments, harassment, publishing others' private information, and conduct that would reasonably be
considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing acceptable behavior. They may edit,
remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they
deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Report abuse privately to a maintainer through the contact options on the maintainer's GitHub profile.
Do not open a public issue containing sensitive personal information. Reports will be reviewed
promptly and handled with respect for the privacy and safety of the reporter.
